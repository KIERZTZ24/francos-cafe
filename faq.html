FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Help & Assistance

Find answers regarding our store hours, delivery coverage, GCash payments, and event booth rentals.

What are your operating hours?
We are open daily from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM at Brgy. Canarvacanan, Binalonan, Pangasinan. You can order online anytime during operating hours for pickup or delivery!
Where do you deliver and what is the delivery fee?
We deliver around Binalonan and neighboring towns. Our standard delivery fee for Binalonan is ₱50.00 flat rate. Free delivery promos are also occasionally available via promo codes!
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept GCash / Maya (Instant QR Code Scan upon checkout) and Cash on Pickup / Delivery (COD).
How do I book a Coffee Booth for events?
You can book our Mobile Coffee Bar directly under the "Event Packages" category on the Menu page! Select between 50 cups (₱5,000), 75 cups (₱7,000), or 100 cups (₱9,000), and place your order.
